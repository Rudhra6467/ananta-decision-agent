The within-quarter shuffle asks whether flagged trades beat unflagged ones by more than chance. **13 notable stock-level results pass the quarter shuffle but fail the within-day shuffle**: the E04 saturation and P01 RSI families. The flagged trades did earn what the registry says, but because of the days they fall on (oversold stocks cluster after broad sell-offs that rebound; extended stocks cluster late in rallies that fade), not because the flagged stocks beat other stocks on the same day. Consequences: (1) as stock-selection filters inside a day they add nothing measurable; (2) the effect may still be usable as a market-timing signal, which is a different hypothesis (an aggregate share of oversold or extended stocks, tested at the market level) and must be registered and tested as such; (3) the REV paper campaign (RSI under 30) is, by this evidence, a market-timing campaign and is read that way.

## Holdout protocol

The survivor list is frozen in manifests/holdout_candidates.json (sha256 fbc3e47aa4018fbc..., 53 candidates). The holdout (2024 onward) is scored once, for exactly these candidates, with their registered rules and the same costs; the threshold there is Bonferroni over the candidate count. A result that does not hold on the holdout is dropped, not re-tuned. New hypotheses added later get their own frozen list.
