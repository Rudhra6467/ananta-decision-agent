## What was walked

- 126 days, 26 Nov 2026 to 31 Mar 2027, at six Toronto times each (07:45, 20:10, 21:00, 22:45, 00:45, 05:00): 756 frozen moments.
- Asked of Ananta's own code at each moment: the live clock (`jarvis/india/livesession.clock`: today in India, whether it is a session, open or closed, the latest session, the next session, and what "tomorrow" means); the next session after each day (four functions: app `answerguide.next_session_after`, records `dayplan.next_session`, `morning.next_session`, `managed.next_session`); the Kite sign-in window named (`morning.next_signin`); the options contract's expiry (`sp1paper.contracts`, from today's instruments file); the expiry words (`desk.expiry_words`); and each India job's Indian time, by replaying launchd day by day with the schedule check at 03:30, 15:00 and 18:00 Toronto rewriting the times.
- The reference, written in the script before it ran: Monday to Friday minus NSE's holiday master, plus 26 Jan 2027 (Republic Day); Nifty weekly expiry each Tuesday, Bank Nifty monthly on the last Tuesday, moved to the session before when that Tuesday is shut; the options line buys the nearest expiry after its day; each job at its time in `jarvis/india/schedule.TARGETS` on every Indian date.

## What agreed

- 744 of the 756 moments (all but 12 on 24-26 Jan 2027): today, session or not, open or closed, latest and next session, and "tomorrow". This includes Christmas (25 Dec), every weekend, the moments after midnight in India while it is still evening in Toronto, and the clock change.
- Every job but one at its Indian time on every Indian date, including Sunday 14 March 2027, when Toronto moves to summer time: the sign-in stays at 06:40 IST (20:10 Toronto becomes 21:10).
- Every option the paper line has bought since 26 Oct (24 decisions) had the right expiry, including November's, moved to Monday 23 Nov by the 24 Nov holiday.
- Bank Nifty's contract expiry on every session day in the walk; Nifty's to 28 Dec. After that, today's instruments file does not list those weekly contracts yet (Zerodha lists them later), so later Nifty days could not be checked. That is not a fault.

## Findings

1. **No 2027 holiday list, so 26 Jan 2027 is treated as a session (fail).** The holiday file on the Mac (`manifests/nse_holidays.json`, fetched nightly) ends on 25 Dec 2026, because NSE has not published 2027 yet. On 25-26 Jan 2027 the live clock says 26 Jan is a session (12 moments wrong), all four next-session functions answer 26 Jan for "after 25 Jan", the sign-in window is named "for the Tue 26 Jan session", and January's expiry is said as "Monday 25 Jan" with no word that it moved from the holiday. Zerodha's instruments already list January's monthly expiry as Mon 25 Jan, which agrees that NSE will be shut. Any other 2027 holiday is unknown until NSE publishes. Nothing checks how far ahead the holiday list reaches. Filed: `prelive-T2-holidays-2027`.
2. **The fact map's 00:05 IST run slips an hour on the night of the clock change (minor).** The 18:00 Toronto schedule check on Saturday 13 March writes the fact map's three times for Sunday 14 March in India, and those straddle the change (two in winter time, one in summer time). So the run meant for 00:05 IST Monday 15 March would run at 23:05 IST Sunday, and the 15:00 check that afternoon puts it right. It is a checker on a Sunday night with no session, so it is minor. Filed: `prelive-T2-factmap-clock-change`.

## Seen, not Ananta's code

The scheduled tasks' own instructions name Toronto times (the sign-in at 20:10, the session jobs 21:00-05:00). After 14 March 2027 those move an hour later (21:10; 22:00-06:00). This readiness run ends by 11 Dec, but any task still running then needs its times read again.
