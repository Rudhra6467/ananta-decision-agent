1. **Ananta's dip trigger already finds his moments.** With W1 it bought within minutes of him at C1 and C2. What it can't do is tell *the* dip from every dip: it also bought all the way down.
2. **His read adds location and context**, which the engine doesn't have: a new 52-week low ~75% under the high, a multi-year price level, panic-level RSI on BTC, ETH and SOL at once, and capitulation volume (C1); a higher low while BTC made a lower low (C2). The engine's "support" is only the last 10 days of 1-hour swings; at C2 it had no support level at all.
3. **The exit is the bigger gap.** Ananta sold each dip buy within about an hour (warning bell under the 1-hour average), and its stops are ~2% away. A 2% stop would have stopped him out at the bottom of C1 (−4.5%) and C3 (−5.2%). His flexible stop under structure is what turned the entries into +56–94%.
4. **C3 is a different trade** (buying strength after the base held), and Ananta's trend entries all failed there with tight stops. His reasons for C3 are still needed.
5. **"Bear weakening" showed up at C2, not C1.** C1 was capitulation (selling still accelerating); the weakening signs (higher low against BTC's lower low) came three weeks later.

## Candidate ingredients (to be tested, not rules)
- Location: at or below the 52-week low; ≥60% under the 52-week high; at a multi-year level.
- Market-wide extreme: daily RSI ≤ 20 on the coin and BTC/ETH together.
- Capitulation: day volume ≥ 2.5× the 90-day median, or an hourly flush with volume ≥ 6×.
- Bear weakening: a higher low on the coin while BTC makes a lower low.
- Position: buy in steps; stop beyond the structure (not ~2%); hold for weeks; size small.
