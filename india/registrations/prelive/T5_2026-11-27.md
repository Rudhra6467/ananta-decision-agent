## A. The options decisions: every choice matches

133 decisions (22 sessions, 26 Oct to 27 Nov) and 1,443 candidates were recomputed. Every check matched:
- the levels from the logged state with the frozen cut-offs;
- each prediction and standard error from the frozen model (sha 52bfef951e1c6556, the one every row names);
- pred_net = pred_gross - cost;
- "qualifies" = pred_net above c times the standard error and above 0;
- the choice, the highest qualifying pred_net;
- c (Nifty 2.5, Bank Nifty 0.5);
- no decision for an index after it traded that day.

25 choices were logged, 24 of them tradable. 0 differences.

## B. The stock picks: every pick matches its family

The 348 picks over 27 decision days (BRK 74, PULL 70, REV 74, CTL 130) each meet their family's definition from their own logged features:
- the top-250 liquidity limit;
- the 20-day breakout on 1.5x volume above the 200-day;
- 5% under the 20-day while above the 200-day;
- RSI(14) under 30;
- the no-chase limits from 30 Oct (the control exempt).

6,999 refusals for "no chase" and "outside the liquid 250" match their reasons. 0 differences.

## C. The fills

- **The rule is followed.** All 23 entries are exactly the open of the minute after the decision row was written. 21 of 23 exits are exactly the price their exit rule names.
- **Two exits cannot be checked** (finding 3). 4 Nov's Bank Nifty exit (BANKNIFTY26NOV55800CE, Rs 783 to Rs 1,352.60 at the 5 Nov open) and 6 Nov's (BANKNIFTY26NOV57500CE, Rs 778.25 to Rs 1,940 at the 9 Nov open) have no recorded candle and no recorded book on the Mac. Kite no longer serves expired contracts: the 9 Nov candle request was refused (HTTP 400), and the 55800 CE is not in today's instruments. Their index moves (+196 bp and +294 bp) make the prices plausible, but together these two trades are about Rs 51,300 of the line's Rs 106,612.
- **Against the book, most fills are outside the bid and ask** (finding 1). Of 41 legs with a book at their minute (23 entries, 18 exits), 4 are inside it.
  - Buys: up to 5.4% above the ask (6 Nov), or up to 2.3% below the bid (25 Nov).
  - Sells: up to 9.6% below the bid (16 Nov exit), or up to 5.8% above the ask (18 Nov Nifty exit).
  - The rule takes the minute candle's first trade. The book was read 6 to 26 seconds into the same minute.
  - On the 18 trades with a book at both ends, the paper fills made Rs 4,047 gross. Buying at the ask and selling at the bid would have made Rs 8,919. So the rule does not flatter the total, but no single fill is a price a live order was offered.
- **The decision's own quote against the entry** (flag 214's case, covered; finding 2). In 14 of 23 entries the option's fill was more than 0.5% above the quote logged at the decision. That includes 8 of the 9 scored 09:15 entries, which were 1.2% to 11.4% above. At 09:15 the logged quote was within about 1% of the 09:15 book on most days.

## Findings

1. **Paper option fills are not prices the book offered.** Filed: flag 306, `prelive-T5-fills-outside-book`.
2. **The live path's limit band would miss most 09:15 trades (touches live money).** The design's limit (the decision price plus 2 ticks plus 0.5%, cancelled after 60 seconds, never chasing) would not have reached the paper fill on 14 of 23 entries, 8 of 9 at 09:15. So live and paper would part on most trades. The design also says the band is "the same slippage the realistic-fill rule uses", but the rule has no band: it is the next minute's open. Filed: flag 305, `prelive-T5-live-limit-band`.
3. **Two exits, about half the options result, cannot be checked.** The scorer keeps no copy of the candle it priced with. Filed: flag 307, `prelive-T5-unverifiable-exits`.
