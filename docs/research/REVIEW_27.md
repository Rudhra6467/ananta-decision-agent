# Review #27 (pre-registration, Oct 11, 2026): stops for dip-style buys

**Why:** every closed brain and zone-touch trade on paper (26 of 26) ended at its stop in the Oct 7-8 dip, and 8 of the brain's 10 open
lab-coin trades are now up. Review #18 found dip buys do best without tight stops. Plan Batch 4a, gap G11.

**Entries (daily candles, 120 lake coins, from 2018):**
- H07: RSI(10) under 30 while the close is above the 200-day average (as live).
- TOUCH200: the day's low reaches the 200-day average while the close holds at or above 97% of it, after closing more than 5% above
  it at some point in the previous 20 days (the 200-day zone, review #16).
Entry at the next day's open; one trade per coin at a time; tier by the 30-day median traded value (A, B, C) as in review #26.

**Exits, all with a 10-day time exit:**
- TIGHT: a stop 1 daily range (ATR 14) under the entry: what the brain and zone touches do today.
- STRUCT: a stop 0.5 ATR under the lowest low of the last 10 days.
- DISASTER: a stop 15% under the entry.
- NOSTOP: the time exit only.
Costs: NDAX 0.20% a side plus the tier's spread floor, as review #26.

**Pass rule (written before the run):** a variant replaces TIGHT only if its average net % per trade beats TIGHT's in BOTH periods
(2018-2023 and 2024-Oct 2026), on tiers A and B together, with at least 30 separate market days in each period, and its worst
single-period average is not below TIGHT's. Among passing variants, the one with the higher 2024-2026 average is used.
If none passes, nothing changes.

**What changes if it passes:** the brain's DIP exit plan and the zone-touch watch use the winning stop.
