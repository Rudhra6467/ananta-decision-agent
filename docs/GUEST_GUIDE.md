# Ananta: a guide for a friend trying the app

**What it is.** Ananta is Madhav's trading assistant ("Jarvis"). It watches 10 crypto coins all day, explains what it sees, and trades
**paper money only** (nothing real) so it can learn from its own record before any real money is used.

**How to open it (iPhone or Android)**
1. Install **Expo Go** from the App Store / Google Play.
2. Open this link on the phone: **exp://app.livetrading247.com** (or open Expo Go, tap "Enter URL manually" and paste it).
3. Log in with the guest email and password Madhav sends you.
4. Every screen shows a **PRACTICE MODE** strip: your orders, alerts and changes go to your own practice book and never touch Madhav's.

**The coins it watches:** Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Cardano, Dogecoin, Avalanche, Bitcoin Cash, Chainlink, Litecoin, XRP.

**The tabs**
- **Home:** today's summary, the activity feed (paper buys and sells, alerts, setups that showed up).
- **Markets:** every coin; zones (price bands that matter), "Your setups" (Madhav's own buy patterns), and Ananta's reasoning: where each coin stops on the rule gates.
- **Portfolio:** the paper books: the Explorer (small $100 ideas), T3 (the trend portfolio) and your practice book.
- **Ananta:** talk or type to the assistant. Tap **"New here"** for starter questions.
- **Evidence:** what it has learned: tests, results, and what is waiting for more live evidence.

**Good first questions** (tap them under "New here", or just ask)
- What's happening here? · What are we doing here? · Show me around
- Which coins are we watching? · Show me the trades · What are the rule gates?
- How do I make a paper trade? · What should I watch today?

**The rule gates** (every idea walks them in order; the first one that fails means no trade)
market allowed (Bitcoin above its 50-day average) → the coin's own trend → at a good zone → a signal → where the idea is wrong (the stop) → can it be sized (1% risk) → not too much in one bet.

**Making a paper trade.** Ask in words, e.g. "buy $100 of Solana". Ananta prepares a card with the price and a stop; nothing happens
until you tap **Confirm**. It goes to your practice book. Ask "how is my practice book doing?" any time.

**What is locked in practice mode:** the kill switch, autopilot, portfolio approvals and the AI news check (they belong to Madhav's real paper books).
