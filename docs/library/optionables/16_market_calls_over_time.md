## 1. How he reads the market week to week

These are the signals he keeps returning to. They are his method as described, not validated rules.

### Option-chain open interest (dominant in 2020–2022)

- **Strike roles.** He treats heavy call writing at a strike as resistance and heavy put writing as support [tn3IDCnC6dc, 81ad8bxpbrA, FBNJsrqlPiQ, KqKOdPHFG-I, VV3vue5PaQo].
- **Straddle concentration.** A cluster of short straddles at one strike marks the likely pin or pivot for expiry [cwcwg2d8pPU, yS4PWW8mfsE, MusX9A1juic, 3VTymF8_eww].
- **Unwinding.**
  - Call unwinding plus put addition reads bullish [QED0exJOqYc, cJpUydwXnIw, o2JJ5p03cyc, s7M-kuYOROU].
  - Put unwinding plus call building reads bearish [CfriQ26BKrs, -wVTqlE51C8].
- **PCR as a tilt.**
  - About 0.4–0.6 reads bearish [NwFNA0IjOkc, xf1DJgNy3mQ, 81ad8bxpbrA].
  - About 1 reads indecisive [tn3IDCnC6dc, oQXCIfaJ1fs].
  - About 1.2–1.3 reads constructive [EL1Baky8Uk8, VyF_xG1f6Aw].
  - About 2 reads bullish [QED0exJOqYc].
- **Straddle price as the expected range.**
  - Nifty straddle of about 130–150 points [Fe766Um-ayQ].
  - Bank Nifty straddle of 600–700 points [JPqXyHXfGfg, jp-dpnaLs38].
  - Pricing of far out-of-the-money options [G2Jm-SaT6M8].
- **Timing of OI reads.**
  - He waits for the next-week OI to build before taking a bias [ApP_IR63Ff8, 2jwiR_sR8W4].
  - He says OI levels are less reliable in news-driven, high-VIX phases [N8jDRk63lK0, tn3IDCnC6dc, G2Jm-SaT6M8].

### Chart structure

**Throughout:** support and resistance from repeated touches, and the idea that broken resistance becomes support and vice versa [ujTuECvyPzc, Y_0mKcvRGzo, 2m1GpJzXBt4, hm1ZKfkO9Oo].

**From roughly mid-2023 onward, the weekly reviews become almost entirely price action:**

- Breakouts from all-time highs and their retests.
- The low of a "trend-day" or breakout candle as the invalidation level.
- Mother candle and inside bar setups.
- Channels, double tops, ascending triangles, head-and-shoulders.
- "No follow-through" after a big red candle, read as dip buying.
- Typical examples: [ZbtIFwwTWhQ, z3WtRYijB8U, xpMrVzXpW34, UnT9BHkfr0g, oXZsCNPBUoE, xI4klDv-W9U, F0x5faXXRTI].

**Standard phrasing.** Calls are usually framed as invalidation rules: no bearish view unless a stated level breaks on a daily close [-jtW7ba1bsg, m3vTimQ3HiE, QN-jSz1pB3E, Mt5qSl4j3fQ].

### Flows and breadth

- **FII selling versus DII buying.** Their standoff is his explanation for ranges, and its resolution is his trigger for trend moves [h5XNvyTMSsk, xf1DJgNy3mQ, GEdPl2wK2mY, RFvomGYSt6k].
- **Breadth counts.** Examples: 44 advances versus 6 declines [Y_0mKcvRGzo]; about 80% decliners [5vAHDwZkoHc].
- **Sector participation** [_sEwi7lQuNA, j02d90xtn_U].

### Heavyweights and relative strength

- **HDFC Bank as the gate for Bank Nifty.** This recurs in 2022 and is constant in 2024 [4hAvzzjA2xw, 5PSE_0Yw9sc, ZbtIFwwTWhQ, BT__dPid_rk, WT2kYd3Sofg, F0x5faXXRTI].
- **Weight logic.** IT, banks and Reliance drive Nifty, while metals (about 3% weight) matter little [RFvomGYSt6k]. IT weakness is often given as the Nifty drag [LPGCiis_rlg, xzkqo36gp8c, bxx4CGJ39Ks].
- **Bank Nifty versus Nifty.** He often judges which index is relatively stronger and leans on that one [kmfo3YKUF4Q, FBNJsrqlPiQ, UIf_6_Bg66I, Mt5qSl4j3fQ, c1hz4zsTuvc].

### Global cues and pre-open indicators

- US close, European session and SGX Nifty for the next open [rEX0xkc5Fes, pZPCRg5w9gw, T1cJv2Y62FU, mW37Il_RHNg, 0xrgICpggJI].
- At one point he even reads an inverse relation between the early indicator and the close [hm1ZKfkO9Oo].

### Volatility regime (India VIX)

- **Low VIX:** small moves, which favour sellers and calendars [kaPyJdhVI9c, oO-T8_3l_5g, tTR4X1FsFgE, uoFMqwhG5-4].
- **VIX above 20 to 30:** smaller size, intraday only, no overnight positions [tn3IDCnC6dc, 3MMjcR_ortI, tJEhYTG7_k4].
- **Thresholds he names:**
  - No long futures until VIX is below 25 [PiY8pG6Jox4].
  - Fresh community trade once VIX is below 20 [35_p7TIkaTQ].

### Scheduled events

He consistently expects IV to rise into an event and crush after it:

### Hedging his own calls

- He frequently adds that he could be wrong [EL1Baky8Uk8, xDRqGkiOnN8, Vn40nCE-lLI, 3nZVT1T90R0, 2jwiR_sR8W4].
- He says outright that no one can predict the market [-kE6iIjC1Ys, CZNC0veemEw], even while giving level calls in the same period.

## 3. Which calls can be scored, and how

### A. Directly scoreable: explicit band plus explicit expiry or week

This covers most index calls from 2021 to 2022 and the weekly reviews from 2023 to 2024.

**How to score:**

1. Get the true upload date from each id, and fix the call's window.
2. Score two things separately:
   - (i) whether the index **settled** inside the band at expiry;
   - (ii) whether it **traded** outside the band before expiry, using both intraday high/low and close.
   His wording mixes "settle", "trade" and "not cross", so tag each call by which type it is.
3. For "won't cross X" calls (for example KqKOdPHFG-I, 3VTymF8_eww, G2Jm-SaT6M8, lyQNM8KBec4), score barrier touch.

**Benchmark:**
- Compare each band with the option-implied range at the time: ATM straddle price, or India VIX scaled to days to expiry.
- He uses the straddle as his own yardstick [Fe766Um-ayQ, jp-dpnaLs38]. The test is whether his bands add information beyond it, and whether OI-cited strikes act as barriers more often than strikes at the same distance with no OI cited.

### B. Conditional or invalidation calls

These are the "bullish unless close below X" form common from 2023 to 2024.

**How to score:**
- Do not score as hit or miss on direction alone.
- Score:
  - (i) the forward return over the stated week, conditional on the level holding;
  - (ii) the move after a break, measured from the break.
- Compare against unconditional weekly returns in the same regime.
- Daily-close versus intraday breach matters; he usually specifies a close.
- Several levels are caption-uncertain: dmZmt1T5bU0, VWpU_gijON8, c1hz4zsTuvc, QIcP9ScvavE, -jtW7ba1bsg, UnT9BHkfr0g, xpMrVzXpW34. Flag these or drop them.

### Practical cautions for the program

- **Upload dates are not in these notes.** "Next session" calls cannot be anchored without the real date. Use the explicit expiry dates where given.
- **Caption errors affect some numbers.** Examples: the size of the "15%" crash in k6bDh3ONgz0, the 22,700 versus 33,000 support in Vn40nCE-lLI, crude at "1.5 dollars" in PiY8pG6Jox4, and "2700" for 20,700 in -jtW7ba1bsg. Confirm against the videos before scoring.
- **Count by distinct view, not by call line.** Many weeks have several overlapping calls from consecutive videos, so the 614 lines overstate how many independent forecasts there are.
