## Review 39: FAIL
The thirds core had to match the all-in core's year-over-fall in both periods. It did in 2018-2023 (1.27 against 0.85: a shallower fall for
a little less return) and did not in 2024-2026 (0.21 against 0.46: it made 13% where all-in made 34%, with a similar fall). It ended above
its starting money in both. By the frozen mark this is a fail. As decided before the run, the choice (keep thirds, return to all-in, or
something else) is Madhav's; nothing live changed.

Read plainly: thirds buys less and later. In long, steady rises (2019-2021) that cost little and saved a deep fall. In the choppy market of
2024-2026 it kept the book thinly invested (20% on an average day) and missed most of the gain.

## Review 40: FAIL (a near miss)
Base version by the pre-set rule: thirds (the better year-over-fall in 2018-2023). The step (a first buy only after the dial has been open
two closes in a row) had to cut entries by a quarter in both periods without lowering year-over-fall. It cut entries by 31.7% in 2018-2023
and by 19.2% in 2024-2026: under the mark in the second period. Year-over-fall was not lower in either. By the frozen mark this is a fail and
the step is not offered as a rule.

Beside, not deciding: the step raised return and year-over-fall in all four rows (both versions, both periods) and lowered cost in all
four. That is a reason to re-test it once more under a fresh pre-registration, not a reason to adopt it now.
