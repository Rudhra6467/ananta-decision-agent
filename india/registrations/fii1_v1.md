# FII1: foreign investor (FPI) flows (registered 26 Oct 2026, before any run)

Madhav asked (India chat, 26 Oct): research FII and their influence on stock prices.

- **Data:** NSDL's daily FPI equity net flows, 2000–2023 (filled on 25 Oct), and the Nifty.
- **Timing:** a day's flow counts only from the next session. The return is then measured from the session after that.
- **What is tested:** sharp outflow days and weeks (bottom decile against the last three years) against weaker Nifty returns over 1, 5 and 20 sessions. Sharp inflow days and weeks (top decile) against stronger returns.
- **Judged:** market-timing blocks (quarters), development 2003–2017 and validation 2018–2023.
- **Bar:** family "flows" (which already holds C06's 4 rows), frozen at k = 12 (z about 2.87).
- **Descriptive only (TWT-H48):** how much the Nifty moves with same-day FPI flows, by year, before and after the SIP era. This is a measurement, not a trading rule and not a verdict.
- No paper rule changes because of this study.
