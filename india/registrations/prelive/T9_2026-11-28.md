## Part A: index days against their members (NIFTY 50 and NIFTY BANK, 2000-2023)

- **Method.** Index day moves come from the Kite index candles (`silver/kite_index/day`, from 2003). No point-in-time member lists exist before Oct 2026, so the members are a proxy: for NIFTY 50, the day's 50 most traded stocks; for NIFTY BANK, the listed banks among the day's 250 most traded. Each is weighted by 60-session median turnover, with returns from the adjusted research bars.
- **Found: one day.** NIFTY BANK, 27 Feb 2009: -14.75% while the bank members rose 1.11% (27 members). NIFTY 50 had none.
- **Explained.** The Kite NIFTY BANK candles have no rows from 16 to 26 Feb 2009: the row before 27 Feb is 13 Feb, 9 sessions earlier. The -14.75% is two weeks of falling (4,565.85 to 3,892.40) read as one day. Over the same two weeks, the indexes that kept their rows fell about the same: Nifty PSU Bank -14.1%, Nifty Private Bank -15.2% (Nifty 50 -6.3%). So the 27 Feb level is right; only the missing days are at fault. This is flag 248's day (the stress test of 24 Nov).
- **The same fault, not caught by the 2% rule.** The NIFTY BANK candles also have no rows from 7 to 11 Dec 2009 (4 Dec is followed by 14 Dec; 5 sessions). The 1-7 Oct 2014 gap in both indexes is NSE holidays (2, 3 and 6 Oct), not a fault.
- **Not marked for studies.** Only the stress test skips the day (`india/stress.py` OPT_EXCLUDE). About 40 study modules read the Kite index candles, and none skips a return taken across a gap.

## Part B: Kite against NSE (stocks, 2003-2023)

- Kite's daily candles are adjusted for later splits and bonuses and NSE's bhavcopy is not, so raw closes differ by design: 54% of 1.27 million pairs are over 2% apart. The fair comparison is the day's return: Kite close to close against NSE's close over its own prev_close, which NSE adjusts on an ex-date.
- 1,273,628 stock-days compared (115 more left out where a value is not a number). 4,761 (0.37%) differ by over 2 points, and 27,362 by over 0.5 points.
- 339 of the 4,761 fall on a corporate-action date on file: explained.
- **4,422 stock-days across 249 stocks are not explained**, mostly 2003-2009 (765 in 2003, 641 in 2008, 26 in 2023). The worst look like Kite glitches (JMFINANCIL 10 Oct 2006, Kite +22,036%) or a corporate action missing from the factor file (NMDC 27 Oct 2022, Kite +85.7% and NSE -20.2%, on the NMDC Steel demerger; no NMDC action after 2008 is on file).
- **Risk to studies is low but not proven.** Kite's daily stock candles are read by the quality, lineage, catalog and intraday code, not by the studies, which use NSE's bhavcopy through the adjusted bars. The missing corporate action may also sit in those adjusted bars.
