## Findings

1. **A made-up figure passes when another figure is near it.** IA79's Rs 63,766 is the model's own sum. The number check accepts any rupee figure within 2% of one of the 343 rupee figures in the facts, and 62,576 and 64,797 are there for other reasons. Filed: flag 300, `prelive-T7-near-figure`.
2. **Dates and days are not checked.** The number check skips dates by design, and the today-block check does not compare a weekday or date said for a review, a pick or a session with the calendar and the facts' own dates (IA28, IA23). Filed: flag 301, `prelive-T7-dates-unchecked`.
3. **Figures that disagree inside one answer, or with an earlier answer, are not checked.** Six of the misses are of this kind: a count said two ways, two levels for one rate, a total that disagrees with an earlier turn. The guard reads one answer against the facts only. Filed: flag 302, `prelive-T7-inconsistent-figures`.

The rebuild has one limit: the parts of the fact page that are not in the read model (the trials list, the book's own views) are today's, not the answer's day. That can only make the guard find more figures, not fewer, so it does not explain a miss.
