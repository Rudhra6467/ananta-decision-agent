# Review 40: does a confirmation step stop the flip-flops at the dial's line?

Written 10 Nov 2026, before the data is touched, with review 39 and under the same lift of Rule B (Madhav, 10 Nov 2026 08:41 Toronto).
No live rule changes on this result without his separate yes.

## 1. Why
In the live book the dial flipped five times in 12 days (26 Oct to 6 Nov 2026). Two re-entries a fraction of a percent above the 50-day
average were sold within one or two nights and cost C$9.15 of a C$500 book (the outsider's review, 10 Nov 2026). Five flips are too few to
design a rule from, so one simple, common confirmation is tested on eight years, once.

## 2. The tier that decides
The core only (BTC 0.40, ETH 0.20). One row decides: the base version WITH the confirmation against the same version WITHOUT it. The base
version is the one named by review 39's section 5 (the better CAGR / |max drawdown| in DISCOVERY).

## 3. The test
Same data, fills, costs, periods and starting money as review 39.
**The confirmation (one value, not tuned):** with no core held, a first buy needs the dial open on two daily closes in a row (today's and
yesterday's). Nothing else changes: exits, adds and trims are as in the base version.
Reported beside, never deciding: the other version with and without the step.

## 4. Pass marks (frozen)
The step passes when, in BOTH periods:
1. it cuts the number of core entries (a buy from no core) by a quarter or more, and
2. the CAGR / |max drawdown| with the step is not below the one without it.
One look. Anything else is a fail.

## 5. What each outcome changes (decided now)
- Pass: the step is offered to Madhav as a candidate rule with the figures. It enters the live book only on his yes, with the pin history.
- Fail: the step is not offered; the flip-flops stay a known cost of the dial, and the write-up says so.
Nothing live changes on the result itself.

## 6. Not in this review
Other confirmations (a buffer above the line, three closes, weekly closes), exits, the short-dip rule, intraday data.
