# Market calls over time

This chapter collects the market-level and sector-level calls Raunak A made in his weekly and periodic reviews, as recorded in the notes. Everything below is his opinion or method at the time. None of it is established fact; each item is a hypothesis for the research program to test.

**Dating caveat.** Video dates come from YouTube's relative labels ("5 years ago", "3 months ago") read as of early October 2026, so every date is approximate. A label of "N years ago" can mean anywhere from N to N+1 years back. Where a call names its own date or a datable event (a budget, an RBI meeting, an index level), I use that to place it more precisely and say so. Some placements within the "1 year ago" window (roughly late 2024 to October 2025) remain uncertain, and I flag those.

## 1. How he reads the market week to week

These are the signals that recur across his reviews. The notes record that he uses them, not how well they work.

### Breadth as the main health gauge
He quotes the percentage of stocks above the 50 DMA and the 200 DMA almost every time. Usually the universe is the Nifty 500 or the total market.

- **Healthy but narrow:** 83% above the 50 DMA but only 46% above the 200 DMA [Jpu536_AmGc].
- **Poor:** 29–30% above the 200 DMA [yYwiSDVwgyA]; 21–22% overall [Sb6KTt64YKE].
- **Recovering:** 39% above the 200 DMA [NtpyZH5cfaE]; 65% above the 50 DMA and 58% above the 200 DMA [S--7ta4FmbY].

He also tracks the share of stocks in a higher-high/higher-low structure: 43% [Jpu536_AmGc], about 52% [YFsvQXZZt1g], 35% [sIo5XQqZ0b0].

A recurring rule of thumb: he wants roughly half or more of stocks above the 200 DMA before trusting a broad trend [Jpu536_AmGc, WI0tdUjH_Vg, SbtFCxbIB1w].

Later reviews add two more measures:
- A count of sectors in the "leading quadrant", such as 36 of 56 [6K1LOJqZYEg].
- A narrowing of his rank A/B stock lists [N2dUTcb9CzQ].

### Moving-average pivots on indices
The 50 DMA and 200 DMA on Nifty, Bank Nifty, Nifty 500 and Smallcap 250 act as his line in the sand.

- A first close below the 50 DMA ended an up-leg, and he cut positions sharply [8QeR8FmYYg4].
- Bank Nifty losing its 50 DMA pivot was a warning for large caps [rhiXs5Kh_0g, r63sarc_qV8].
- Bear-versus-correction calls are pinned to specific pivots, such as the low of the election-day Nifty 500 candle [hKnYYRpd63s, M4Z-Gi4gGag, elpQ8wyLZLQ].

### Stage analysis (Weinstein)
Indices and sectors are labelled Stage 1 to Stage 4, and this labelling is constant from 2019 onward.

- Silver in Stage 2 [G2gjQUJRfJM].
- Nifty IT in a Stage 1 base, turning Stage 2 only above about 32,000 [NUDjIohACWs].
- Nifty 500 in Stage 3 throughout much of 2026 [r63sarc_qV8, CFJNeCPMQ1o, YFsvQXZZt1g, 8QeR8FmYYg4, rhiXs5Kh_0g].
- Hotels in Stage 4 [rbSj4pOg3_I].

### Relative strength and ratio charts
He uses RS lines against the Nifty or a commodity index to rank sectors.

- Private Bank vs Nifty 50 [Jpu536_AmGc].
- Silver vs the CRB index [G2gjQUJRfJM].
- Metals RS breakout vs Nifty [JJEB-UZgK4E].
- He would not buy Wipro on a price breakout without an RS breakout [Ux_uuOj-gKs].

He also uses size-ratio charts (small/mid vs large; large-mid 250) to decide which capitalisation bucket to favour [Qu49zHwEGwo, SbtFCxbIB1w, x2RbjJp1eCI].

### Sector rotation lists
Almost every review ends with leaders, setting-up sectors and laggards. Section 2 tracks how those lists moved. Later reviews add a near-term versus core-trend sector map [6K1LOJqZYEg].

### Bottoming and topping frameworks
- **O'Neil-style:** "day zero" low, follow-through day within about 5 sessions, then a wide-range volume candle by about day 10 [B1ynZNvqesE]. He also uses the undercut-and-rebound idea [r63sarc_qV8, -qQntPmfe-s].
- **Intermediate bottom confirmation:** Nifty must take out recent 75-minute pivots with a higher-high/higher-low structure [-qQntPmfe-s].
- **Correction-stage sequence:** breadth damage, then panic selling, then a sustained rally that needs a trigger [iWa7MbGawvU, V41BUpPK3E4].
- **Distance from the 200 DMA:** an intermediate bottom often comes 8–14% below it [Sb6KTt64YKE].
- **India VIX:** a final low may come near 32–33 [Sb6KTt64YKE]. He reads a very low VIX as either a source of random pullbacks (about 9.3) [SbtFCxbIB1w] or not a warning without a catalyst (about 10.45) [kwbfXjRtEkk].

### Macro and cross-asset overlays
These become much heavier from 2024 on.

- **Crude:** a supply zone; specific levels at 88, 99, 100, and 116–130 [yYwiSDVwgyA, Sb6KTt64YKE, r63sarc_qV8, YFsvQXZZt1g].
- **Dollar index:** 95–105 range; 110 and 120 thresholds [hwOw2HaLUcQ, zHzq3uAFJFs, M4Z-Gi4gGag, 8QeR8FmYYg4].
- **USD/INR** [e34G88-BKI4, zHzq3uAFJFs, dpsU7JvHiMA].
- **US yields and the Fed** [hwOw2HaLUcQ, SuYHqw5zA8U, YFsvQXZZt1g].
- **Nasdaq vs its 50 DMA** [0nbQs92kYuY, sIo5XQqZ0b0, 8QeR8FmYYg4].
- **FII flows** [YVGWKmpHStM, 6K1LOJqZYEg].
- **The event calendar:** budget, RBI, Fed, US elections, tariff deadlines [M4Z-Gi4gGag, hKnYYRpd63s, B8HZvUkY5sA, rhiXs5Kh_0g].

### Chart-pattern language
- **Early era (2018–2021):** daily and weekly Heikin Ashi [e34G88-BKI4], Ichimoku [2crnCCz4Y3s], "initiation candles" [FtxgOtPYqVA, e34G88-BKI4], outside bars [_AiSzhs67J4], demand/supply zones and opening-range breakouts [hk4d8AxbQ3A, dV4MiNaAenI, 1klATqC7oks].
- **Later:** volatility contraction patterns (VCP) [YFsvQXZZt1g, sIo5XQqZ0b0, 0Hnffbj5pgE] and cup-and-handle [yEKNcJ4EH0U, Pm_1kb8Fku0].

### How his method changed
- **Before about 2021**, the reviews in the notes are mostly single-stock setups with explicit price levels: Escorts, TCS, ACC, L&T, Dr Reddy's and others [4CzLWJxP4UM, -SC5vcQ1NuE, CXJZMbE_d5o].
- **From 2023 on**, they shift to breadth, sector rotation, macro and multi-quarter timing calls.
