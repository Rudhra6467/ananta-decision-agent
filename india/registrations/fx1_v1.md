# FX1: the rupee against the dollar (registered 26 Oct 2026, before any run)

Madhav asked (India chat, 26 Oct): evaluate rupee versus dollar against stock prices, to see how it is influencing them.

- **Data:** USD/INR is the Federal Reserve's daily New York rate (FRED DEXINUS), already in the macro table from 1990. A value is used from the next Indian session.
- **What is tested:**
  - **A sharp weak-rupee week (top decile of 5-session moves) or day.** Are the Nifty and Nifty Bank weaker over 5 and 20 sessions, and do the exporters (Nifty IT, Nifty Pharma) beat the Nifty?
  - **TWT-H42, restated on the rupee.** Is the Nifty weaker over 21 and 63 sessions when USD/INR is up 5%+ over 126 sessions, or breaks above its 3-year high?
- **Judged:** market-timing blocks (quarters), development 2003–2017 and validation 2018–2023.
- **Bar:** a new family "fx", k = 10 (z about 2.81).
- **Written down first:** the rupee drifts weaker over the long run, so "a 3-year high" is common. That is part of the claim as stated.
- No paper rule changes because of this study.
