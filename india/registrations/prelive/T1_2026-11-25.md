## Findings

1. **The one-overnight rule skips every Nifty trade so far (4 of 4).** On each of those days Bank Nifty was already held overnight from an earlier decision point. Together the 4 Nifty trades made Rs +3,393 on paper. Live, the line would be almost Bank Nifty only. The rule is Madhav's of 24 Nov and stays. This is said so the live record is read against the right paper record.
2. **Index-level stops cannot be held by the broker.** 4 of the 23 decisions exit on a stop set on the index (ATRSTOP, ORSTOP), not on the option. A broker-side order (GTT) triggers on the option's own price, so live, these exits depend on the Mac watching the index. This is not a broker refusal, but the live path has no answer for it yet. Filed for the live path and for T13 (the missed exit).
3. **The margin calculator** (BANKNIFTY26DEC63400CE, 1 lot, buy, NRML, limit Rs 1,663): Rs 49,890 blocked, exactly 30 × Rs 1,663, plus Rs 45.58 charges. The money needed is the premium itself. At that night's mark the lot cost more than at the decision (Rs 49,890 against Rs 44,806).
