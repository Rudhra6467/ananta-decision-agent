# Short selling and bear markets

## 1. What he teaches here and how it fits his method

Raunak's teaching on the short side has two layers, and they come from different periods.

**The older videos (roughly 6–8 years ago)** treat shorting as the mirror image of his long methods. Each of these has a short version:
- Heikin Ashi candle reading
- Ichimoku Kumo breakouts
- sector and stock relative strength (RS)
- ADX/20-EMA pullbacks
- candlestick and coil-spring patterns

These shorts are gated by a bear-regime filter: Stage 4, or price below a long moving average.

**The recent videos (roughly the last 2 years, down to 7 days ago)** take a firm long-only stance. Their message:
- Do not short corrections.
- Do not write options.
- Cut long exposure instead, research leaders, and wait for the market to stabilise.

The two layers share a thread that appears even in the old material. He says shorts rarely work (about 2 in 10), and he steers beginners to long trades or to puts. So in his overall method, shorting is a narrow, conditional tool that he has increasingly discouraged. His "bear market" playbook today is mainly defensive.

## 2. The ideas

### 2.1 Current stance: long-only, defend rather than short

**What it is.** Do not short the market or stocks, even in sharp falls.
- In a sharp fall, the right action is to cut losing longs and some profitable longs [d3-OMTGOGx8].
- Treat corrections as preparation time. Research the best stocks and hold the list aside until the market stabilises, rather than chasing the next 2–3% down [elpQ8wyLZLQ, 20BxPDdAZAE, T4849hhdwTo].
- He calls shorting and option writing in corrections traps that pay pennies [20BxPDdAZAE].

**His reasons:**
- Shorting works only about 2 times in 10 [d3-OMTGOGx8, RVbze1duD-U], or about 1 in 10 in another video [T4849hhdwTo]. These are his estimates; no data is shown.
- It leads to overtrading [d3-OMTGOGx8].
- It damages psychology [elpQ8wyLZLQ].
- Reversals from lows tend to be sudden and catch shorts off guard [KDSbZ0oW2U0].
- Citing William O'Neill: markets look for reasons to rise, and a short-selling mindset blinds you to RS leaders on the long side. He also says few people can run shorts and longs at once [rhiXs5Kh_0g].
- Even when the market is below its 50- and 200-day averages, he acknowledges shorting is the only trading play. He still declines to teach it, because it requires futures and options (F&O), which he never encourages [Sb6KTt64YKE].

**Earlier version of the same caution.** Eight years ago he already told beginners to use his tools long-only. He suggested put options for anyone wanting short exposure [uFFAAZNSozg].

**Clarity and frequency.** High clarity. This is the most repeated theme in the chapter: 8 videos, concentrated in the last 2 years.

### 2.2 Long-side defence in falls

**What it is:**
- Exit or trim longs, including some winners, during sharp declines [d3-OMTGOGx8].
- Exit longs when a run of red Heikin Ashi candles with no upper shadow appears [uqEFppHC4Jw].
- Use the correction to build a watchlist for the next swing up [elpQ8wyLZLQ, 20BxPDdAZAE, T4849hhdwTo].

**Gaps.** No numeric exposure-cut rules (how much to cut, at what index level) appear in these notes.

### 2.3 Regime gate: when a short is permissible at all

All the older short setups require a confirmed downtrend. The specific filter varies by video:

| Filter | Source |
|---|---|
| Stock in Stage 4 and clearly below its 150-day average; never short in Stage 2 or above the 150-day | [G2gjQUJRfJM] |
| Broad market below its 200 DMA before shorting weak-RS stocks | [488FDla8kuc] |
| Price below the 100-day average (low-clarity video, no worked examples) | [H0XAVFBdbzw] |
| Market below its 50- and 200-day averages (only as an observation) | [Sb6KTt64YKE] |
| Broad market trend down and broad-market RS confirming | [RVbze1duD-U] |
| Coil-spring shorts only in a confirmed downtrend | [VqoLpEvre04] |

The moving-average length is inconsistent across videos (100, 150, 200, 50+200). There is no single canonical gate.

### 2.4 What not to short

- **High P/E alone.** Valuations can get more expensive [G2gjQUJRfJM].
- **A big short-term run-up alone.** Do not short without a Stage 4 breakdown [G2gjQUJRfJM].
- **A stock that has already collapsed.** In his example the stock was already down about 78%. He says typical short trades only make 11–16%, so the reward-to-risk is poor that late [uFFAAZNSozg].
- **Near an intermediate bottom.** Avoid shorting or writing calls when:
  - the bearish view is consensus,
  - price is near known support, and
  - volatility is elevated.

  Over about the next 6 months, he says reward-to-risk then favours longs, though momentum can still push price lower. He cites call writers losing heavily on a sharp rebound [-qQntPmfe-s]. This is from 5 months ago.
- **The first drop.** Wait for a weak retracement after the initial break [e34G88-BKI4].

### 2.5 Heikin Ashi strength reading for shorts

This is the most detailed short-side material. It comes mostly from one 7-year-old video [L99LgSdCDD8], with support from [IHqkSPVCxko, uqEFppHC4Jw, e34G88-BKI4].

**Initiation candles are required.**
- Short only after wide-range bearish initiation candles appear at the start of the move.
- A decline built from narrow bearish candles, with no initiation candle at the start, is a weak downtrend. Do not short it, however many continuation candles follow [L99LgSdCDD8].
- Down moves tend to come on wider-range candles, so wait for confirmation [IHqkSPVCxko].

**Template of a strong downtrend** [L99LgSdCDD8]:
1. The first three HA candles are wide-range initiation candles.
2. Continuation candles follow consistently, with no indecision candles.
3. The first indecision candles appear only after a 5–7% decline.
4. A sideways consolidation then follows.

**Confirmation must be prompt for shorts** [L99LgSdCDD8].
- For longs, confirmation may arrive 2–3 candles late and still work, because demand can come in late.
- For shorts, strength must be visible from the start and checked at each stage.

**Reading supply strength** [L99LgSdCDD8].
- Judge two things together: wide bearish candles, and narrow or indecision bullish candles on rebounds.
- In his example, rebounds came on narrow bullish candles and the decline resumed on new wide bearish candles.

**Counter-trend candles** [IHqkSPVCxko].
- Ignore small indecision candles inside a downtrend.
- React only to wide-range counter-trend candles that confirm a decisive shift.

**Red HA candle with no upper shadow** [uqEFppHC4Jw].
- It marks a strong downtrend.
- Hold the short, do not book profit, possibly add, and exit longs.
- Weak red candles with little range did not follow through.

**Index application** [e34G88-BKI4]. Bank Nifty short considered only if:
1. price moves below 31,600 (a past breakout level), and
2. strong downside initiation candles form on the weekly chart, and
3. a weak retracement back up follows; the short is taken then, not on the first drop.

### 2.6 Ichimoku shorts

**Short Kumo breakout** [2crnCCz4Y3s]:
- Price must be below the cloud.
- Senkou A and Senkou B must both be falling.
- No short while the cloud lines are flat or a twist is only forming.
- Beginners short only after the twist, with both lines clearly pointing down. Experienced traders may go earlier (that trigger is undefined).

**Confirmation** [2crnCCz4Y3s]:
- Chikou below price.
- Tenkan and Kijun both trending lower.
- Otherwise avoid the trade.

**Bearish chart profile** [153pJZk-ixo]:
- Price below the cloud.
- Senkou A below Senkou B.
- Price below Tenkan and Kijun.
- Chikou below price.

With this profile, avoid buying and prefer shorts.

**Intraday stock shorts** [xzR6bwNgb18]: use 10- or 15-minute charts, not 5-minute.

**Clarity.** High clarity and codable, but taught in only 3 older videos.

### 2.7 Relative-strength shorts

**Top-down mirror** [488FDla8kuc]:
1. Benchmark below its 200 DMA.
2. Rank sectors by RS and pick the weakest sectors.
3. Short the weakest-RS stocks within them.

**Stock setup** [RVbze1duD-U]:
- Lower highs in price and lower highs in the RS line.
- Only when the broad market is in a downtrend with market RS confirming.
- In the same video he says shorts work only about 2 in 10.

### 2.8 Pullback and retracement entries

**ADX / 20-EMA mirror** [LahSe1vu1kY]:
1. ADX(14) above 30 and −DMI above +DMI.
2. Price is below the 20-day EMA and rallies up to it.
3. Mark the low of the candle that touches the 20 EMA.
4. Short when that low breaks, on the next or a later session.
5. Stop above the recent swing high. The nearest swing high is the conservative choice; the major swing high is the aggressive one.

**50% retracement** [d7cwBnW78XI]:
- In a down-swing, a rally to 50% of the fall tends to act as resistance; sellers win there.
- Once the 50% area gives way, the decline continues steadily.
- 38.2% or 61.8% may also serve.

**Stochastic** [H0XAVFBdbzw]:
- Short when price is below the 100-day average and Stochastic is above 70.
- The mirror (narrow-range up HA candles in the bounce) is only implied.
- Low clarity, no worked example.

### 2.9 Pattern shorts

**Bearish kicker** [QWcb7yxYAxk]:
- Context: an uptrend.
- Candle 1 is a strong bullish candle.
- Candle 2 opens near candle 1's low and closes with a strong bearish body.
- Short by candle 2's close; stop at candle 1's high.

This is the only short in the notes that does not require a prior downtrend.

**Inverted coil spring (distribution)** [VqoLpEvre04]:
- Rare; trade only in a confirmed downtrend.
- Enter on a break of the A-C-E support line, or after the wide-range HA candles that follow the break. Add on those candles.
- Target: entry minus the A-B distance. His example: 8300 entry, A-B about 300, target about 8000.
- In strong momentum he expects 2–3 times A-B. Book out if short-term momentum becomes excessive.

**Gap-down with RSI range** [HHkcCy5LrCM]:
- Take a gap down when RSI has been in the bearish range: about 20–60, capped near 60.
- Gap downs after RSI was in the 40–80 range tended to fail or fill.

## 3. How a program would test it

1. **Long-only vs. long/short in corrections.** Claim: shorts win about 1–2 in 10 [d3-OMTGOGx8, T4849hhdwTo, RVbze1duD-U].
   - Over NSE corrections (index down at least 10% from high), compare the win rate and expectancy of breakdown shorts against long-only with exposure cuts.
   - Data: daily OHLC for the Nifty 500 and indices, futures-roll costs, and correction dates.
   - Also check whether his 1–2-in-10 estimate holds.

2. **Regime gate.** Run identical short signals (e.g., new 50-day low) under three conditions:
   - (a) unconditional
   - (b) stock below its 150-day MA in Stage 4
   - (c) benchmark below its 200 DMA

   Compare 20- and 60-day forward returns. Also compare 100, 150 and 200-day filters, since his videos differ [G2gjQUJRfJM, 488FDla8kuc, H0XAVFBdbzw].

3. **HA initiation filter.**
   - Define an initiation candle as an HA bearish candle with range at least 1.5× its 20-bar average range and no upper shadow.
   - Compare shorts entered after at least one such candle at the start of a decline against declines made only of narrow candles.
   - Also test prompt confirmation (within 1 bar) against delayed confirmation (2–3 bars) for shorts [L99LgSdCDD8, uqEFppHC4Jw].

4. **Ichimoku short gate.** Compare:
   - close below both spans with both falling, plus Chikou below price and falling Tenkan and Kijun,
   - against close below the cloud alone.

   Run on daily data, and on 10/15-minute vs. 5-minute data for the intraday variant [2crnCCz4Y3s, 153pJZk-ixo, xzR6bwNgb18].

5. **Sector-RS shorts.** When the benchmark is below its 200 DMA, short the bottom-decile RS stocks in the bottom-3 RS sectors, rebalanced monthly. Measure returns against the benchmark net of borrow and futures costs [488FDla8kuc, RVbze1duD-U].

6. **ADX/20-EMA short.** Run the exact rules in 2.8 on daily data. Report expectancy in R (risk units) for nearest vs. major swing-high stops [LahSe1vu1kY].

7. **Intermediate-bottom caution.** Compare 6-month outcomes of short futures or short calls opened when:
   - India VIX is in its top quintile and price is within 3% of a prior pivot low,
   - versus other conditions [-qQntPmfe-s].

8. **Overextension.** Compare short returns when the stock is already down at least 70% from its high against shorts at a 20–40% drawdown [uFFAAZNSozg].

## 4. Transfer

**Usable as-is in US and TSX markets.** All the chart logic is market-neutral:
- HA candle reading
- Ichimoku conditions
- RS line and sector-RS ranking
- ADX/20-EMA
- 50% retracement resistance
- bearish kicker
- RSI range
- Stage 4 and moving-average gates

US and Canadian traders can short cash equities overnight (borrow permitting), so his main practical objection does not apply. The F&O objection is about Indian cash-market shorting being intraday-only.

**Needs adapting:**
- **Index levels.** The Bank Nifty 31,600 level is purely local; the structure (break of a prior breakout level, then a weak retest) transfers.
- **Volatility gauge.** Use VIX or VIXC in place of India VIX.
- **Sector rankings.** Use GICS or TSX sector indices. The TSX's narrow sector mix (financials, energy, materials heavy) makes "weakest sector" baskets thin.
- **Costs.** Borrow fees, hard-to-borrow lists, short-sale restrictions on down days (US Rule 201), and dividend payments on shorts.
- **Instruments.** Inverse ETFs and puts are the closest analogue to his "use puts" advice.

**India-only:**
- His reasoning that shorting forces F&O participation.
- His intraday-only cash-shorting constraint.
- His 1–2-in-10 success estimates, which come from his own Indian experience and are untested elsewhere.

## 5. Gaps and doubts

**His stance changed, or at least hardened, over time.**
- The 6–8-year-old videos teach multiple codable short setups.
- The 1–2-year-old and recent videos reject shorting outright and decline to teach it.

Even the old material carried the 2-in-10 warning and beginner long-only advice. So the setups may never have been his own primary practice. The notes do not tell us whether he ever traded them systematically.

**Numbers conflict.**
- Success rate: 1 in 10 [T4849hhdwTo] vs. 2 in 10 [d3-OMTGOGx8, RVbze1duD-U].
- Regime moving average: 100, 150, 200, or 50+200 days.

**No evidence is offered** for any short-side claim: no win rates, samples, or backtests.

**Short-side exits are mostly missing.**
- Stops exist only for the kicker, ADX and coil-spring setups.
- Targets exist only for the coil spring.
- There is no position sizing for shorts and no rules for covering into sudden reversals, despite his warning about them.

**Undefined terms.**
- "Weak retracement" [e34G88-BKI4].
- "Experienced traders may short earlier" [2crnCCz4Y3s].
- "Wide range" and "narrow range" have no numeric threshold.

**Thin sources.**
- [H0XAVFBdbzw] is low-clarity: rules are mentioned without examples.
- The coil-spring short is called rare.
- The HA downtrend template rests on a single video.

**Not covered:**
- Bear-market long strategies beyond "wait and research."
- Hedging.
- How to tell a correction from a true bear market.
- Option-based shorting details beyond "use puts."
- Defensive sectors.
- Cash allocation levels.
