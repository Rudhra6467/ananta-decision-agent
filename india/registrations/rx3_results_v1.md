# RX3 results: breaking out of a tight base (2000-2023)

Registered 2 Nov 2026 (spec `india/registrations/rx3_v1.json`, committed before the run, records 650db19). Bar k 155, z 3.60. Each group is
measured against all liquid stocks on the same next session (RX1's rule); the average liquid stock loses about 0.22% open to close.

| Question | 2000-17 vs average | 2018-23 vs average | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closed above its 20-day high out of a tight base: next session open to close | -0.01% (t -0.18) | -0.02% (t -0.38) | REJECTED |
| Same, over the next 5 sessions | -0.40% (t -2.59) | -0.12% (t -0.62) | OPPOSITE |
| In a tight base near its top, not yet broken out: next session open to close | +0.27% (t 8.46) | +0.05% (t 2.29) | CONDITIONAL |

**Meaning:**
- The missed "breakout base, intraday run" had no rule knowable at the close: buying the breakout close did not lead to a next-day run, and
  over 5 sessions it did slightly worse.
- Stocks sitting tight near the top of a base lose less than the average stock the next session, consistently, but by 0.05% since 2018:
  less than an intraday round trip's costs (about 0.10%). It describes what holds up, not an entry. No rule changes.
- The three held-into-falls drafts of 31 Oct were already answered by RX1 and RX2 (a faster exit did worse in every family) and are recorded
  as answered, not re-tested.
