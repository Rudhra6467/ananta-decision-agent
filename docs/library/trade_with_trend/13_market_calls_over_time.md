# Market calls over time

**Dating note.** The notes give only relative ages ("8y ago", "5 months ago", "6 hours ago"). All dates below are approximate, counted back from early October 2026. Many videos fall in the same "1 year ago" or "2 years ago" bucket. Within a bucket I ordered them by the events they mention, such as an upcoming budget, a Fed meeting, a stated month or an index level. ## 1. How he reads the market week to week

His toolkit changed over time. That change matters for testing.

**Early videos (about 2018–2021)** are mostly about single stocks and candles:
- He reads "initiation" and wide-range candles, outside bars, opening-range breakouts and demand/supply zones.
- He uses multiple-timeframe agreement (monthly, daily, 30/60-minute) [FtxgOtPYqVA, _AiSzhs67J4, hk4d8AxbQ3A, Tkx2xIboYsw, 1klATqC7oks].
- He uses Heikin Ashi on index trend [e34G88-BKI4] and Ichimoku on gold [2crnCCz4Y3s].
- He uses a 200-day-average "beta filter" for which sector to trade [2Yv0E57_bs4].
- He applies a relative-strength (RS) line breakout rule. A price breakout alone is not enough; the RS line must also break out [Ux_uuOj-gKs].
- Calls usually come with exact price levels and entry-on-pullback zones [4CzLWJxP4UM, -SC5vcQ1NuE, CXJZMbE_d5o, dV4MiNaAenI].

**Later weekly reviews (about 2023–2026)** are built around breadth, stages and sector rotation, with macro overlays. The recurring inputs are:

**Breadth percentages.** He tracks three measures:
- the share of stocks above the 50 DMA;
- the share above the 200 DMA;
- the share in a higher-high/higher-low structure.

He usually measures these on the Nifty 500 or Total Market universe. He treats readings under about 50% above the 200 DMA as a narrow or poor market [SbtFCxbIB1w, WI0tdUjH_Vg, N2dUTcb9CzQ, sIo5XQqZ0b0]. He treats a jump to roughly 65% above the 50 DMA as "expansion" [S--7ta4FmbY]. He also uses ranked A/B stock counts [N2dUTcb9CzQ] and a count of sectors in his "leading" quadrant, for example 36 of 56 [6K1LOJqZYEg].

**Weinstein stages.**
- He labels indices and sectors as Stage 1 (base), 2 (uptrend), 3 (top/distribution) or 4 (decline) [NUDjIohACWs, G2gjQUJRfJM, CFJNeCPMQ1o, rhiXs5Kh_0g].
- "Stage 3 broad market, Stage 2 pockets" is his most frequent 2025–26 description [kwbfXjRtEkk, jcWYGNInYg8, 8QeR8FmYYg4].

**50 DMA and 200 DMA as pivots.**
- A sector pulling back to a rising 50 DMA and basing is his preferred add point [DCG1GO9TGFU, 6K1LOJqZYEg, N2dUTcb9CzQ, sIo5XQqZ0b0].
- An index breaking below its 50 DMA pivot is a warning [rhiXs5Kh_0g].
- The Smallcap 250 closing below its 50 DMA ended a cycle for him, and he cut positions [8QeR8FmYYg4].
- He sometimes measures distance from the 200 DMA as a percentage to estimate a bottom zone [Sb6KTt64YKE].

**Ratio charts and RS lines.** He compares:
- small/mid caps against large caps;
- large-mid 250 against the market;
- Private Bank against Nifty 50;
- sector against Nifty.

He uses these to decide which size bucket and which sectors get money [Qu49zHwEGwo, Jpu536_AmGc, SbtFCxbIB1w, x2RbjJp1eCI, S--7ta4FmbY].

**Bottoming sequence (O'Neil-style).**
- "Day zero" low, then a follow-through day within about 5 sessions, then a wide-range volume candle by about day 10 [B1ynZNvqesE].
- An "undercut and rebound" of a prior low [r63sarc_qV8, -qQntPmfe-s].
- Confirmation only when 75-minute pivots form higher highs and higher lows [-qQntPmfe-s].
- A staged model of breadth damage → panic selling → recovery. He has said panic selling was still pending more than once [iWa7MbGawvU, V41BUpPK3E4].

**Volatility contraction / VCP and cup-and-handle patterns** on indices, sectors, gold and silver [yEKNcJ4EH0U, YFsvQXZZt1g, 0Hnffbj5pgE, sIo5XQqZ0b0].

**Macro overlays, with thresholds he names:**
- **Crude:** Brent pivots at about 88 and 99. Crude sustaining below 99 is his first positive sign. A move to 116–130 would drag even strong pockets. A 75–95 band is "okay but range-bound" [yYwiSDVwgyA, Sb6KTt64YKE, r63sarc_qV8, YFsvQXZZt1g].
- **Dollar index:** 110 and 120–125 as risk lines [zHzq3uAFJFs, M4Z-Gi4gGag]. A 95–105 range [hwOw2HaLUcQ]. A base near 100 [8QeR8FmYYg4].
- **USD/INR:** 72.5–73 as a breakout risk in about 2020 [e34G88-BKI4]. 88.5–91 as the level where FIIs return [zHzq3uAFJFs].
- **US indices:** Nasdaq above about 28,000 [0nbQs92kYuY]. Concentration in AI mega-caps as the main tail risk [LWoEC9OjajE, 8QeR8FmYYg4].
- **India VIX:** a final bottom near 32–33 [Sb6KTt64YKE]. He says low VIX alone is not a warning [kwbfXjRtEkk].
- **Others:** FII flows, US yields, Fed/RBI meetings, the Union budget and US elections.

**Sentiment and his own book.**
- A proprietary sentiment indicator at historic lows suggests a rally within 1–2 months [6qExRlD11sE].
- His "test portfolio" stabilising was the basis for a call [YLAIkbRSnsA].
- He states his cash level, for example about 35% [Qu49zHwEGwo], and how many positions he will hold, for example 3–5 [mim7ylyaN74].

**Habitual hedging language.** He often:
- calls a view a guess;
- says he could be wrong or would admit it publicly;
- leaves the horizon vague, as in "a matter of time" or "dark horse".

This matters for Section 3, because many of these calls cannot be scored.
